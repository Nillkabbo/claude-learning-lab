Skills: the procedure layer

Commands merged into skills — your repeatable how-to, invoked or auto-loaded.

≈ 12 minutes + drill Claude Code Guide Hands-on by design
skills run procedures

0010's verified facts, in practice. .claude/commands/deploy.md and .claude/skills/deploy/SKILL.md both create /deploy; skills are the recommended form (supporting files, relevance auto-loading). Precedence: enterprise > personal > project, and a skill beats a same-named command.

  • $ARGUMENTS and $1 substitute; stacking works (/a /b 123 loads both).
  • Frontmatter that matters: allowed-tools — a per-turn grant that clears on your next message; context: fork — an isolated subagent run; disable-model-invocation: true — humans only.
  • Skills live in the project, in ~/.claude, in plugins (plugin-name:skill-name), or via managed settings.
