Phase 1 review: mixed retrieval

Eight questions from all four lessons, deliberately out of order — the honest test of what stuck.

≈ 8 minutes Covers lessons 0001–0004 Interleaved on purpose
interleaved retrieval

Blocked practice (all questions on one topic, right after its lesson) produces fluency that feels like mastery and fades by exam day. Mixed practice like this is harder and slower — and builds the retrieval strength that actually lasts. Expect to reach for the answer; the reaching is the point.
