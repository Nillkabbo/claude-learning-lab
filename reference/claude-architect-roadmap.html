How this works

This is a living workspace, not a book you passively read:

  • The foundation layer — the ten laws of Claude, the build-anything playbook (any project, blank page → owned production), and the starter kit (the fundamentals as working code). Your permanent toolkit.
  • The Apex Project — build the whole roadmap with your hands: ten coded milestones, one per phase, offline-first with tests. Start here (the primary track since 2026-08-27).
  • Ask your agent for the next lesson — say "next lesson". It reads your learning records and picks what fits your current level.
  • Each lesson is small — one idea, one win, a quiz with instant feedback, and a primary source to read.
  • Quiz answers are equal length — no guessing from formatting. Retrieval effort is the point.
  • Ask questions anytime — the agent is your teacher. Confusion left unresolved compounds.
  • The glossary is shared vocabulary — every lesson uses these terms the same way.
