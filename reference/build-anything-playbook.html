The checkpoints

  • Ready to leave phase 1 when: every criterion has a number and an owner; every failure has a cost.
  • Phase 2: you can say in one sentence why the losing postures lost.
  • Phases 3–5: the starter kit's modules run your scenarios green — the contract, the tools, the budget hold.
  • Phase 6: every "never" in your design has an enforced layer named after it.
  • Phase 7: every pattern maps to a phase-1 criterion; the multi-agent rung (if bought) has its 15× justification written down.
  • Phase 8: a change that doesn't pass the suite doesn't ship; the bill has arithmetic, not adjectives.
  • Phase 9: a stranger could take the handoff package and operate it.
What this is The Apex Project (in project/) is the gym where you build these muscles; this playbook is how you use them on YOUR next project. Copy the starter kit, walk the phases, and check yourself against the ten laws at every gate.
